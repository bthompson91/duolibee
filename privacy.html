DUOLIBEE Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026.

DUOLIBEE is a browser-based Tagalog learning app with lessons, vocabulary reviews, Wikipedia quizzes, optional Google Drive progress syncing, and optional Google-signed English translations. Contact: bthompson91@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The app uses browser localStorage to store lesson completion, XP, review schedules, streaks, quiz answers, Wikipedia vocabulary, settings, and an unfinished lesson session. It also stores synchronization metadata, including a Google account identifier, the last synchronized progress copy, file identifiers and sync times. Before a cloud restore, it saves a local progress backup.

English translations may be cached in browser sessionStorage for the current browsing session. This cache contains the Tagalog sentence and its English translation. Closing the browser tab or clearing site data removes it.

Optional Google account and Drive access

Connecting Google is optional. The app requests openid and email to identify the Google account and show its email, and the drive.appdata permission to save and read DUOLIBEE data in that account's private Google Drive application-data folder. It does not request access to your ordinary Drive documents, Gmail, contacts or calendar.

When connected and authorized, learning progress is uploaded as JSON snapshots and can be restored on another device using the same Google account and app. Progress includes the learning and session data described above. The Google email is used for the connection display; the account identifier distinguishes synchronization metadata on the device. OAuth access tokens are held in browser memory and are not included in progress snapshots or deliberately persisted in localStorage. No Google password is collected by DUOLIBEE.

Optional English translations

After you answer an exercise, DUOLIBEE may request an English translation of the associated Tagalog sentence. This feature is optional: lessons and local progress remain fully usable if you do not sign in or if translation is unavailable.

To use translation, DUOLIBEE may request a short-lived Google ID token using the openid scope. The app sends the ID token and the Tagalog sentence to DUOLIBEE's translation gateway over HTTPS. The gateway verifies the token with Google and DUOLIBEE's Client ID before providing a translation.

The translation gateway uses a SHA-256 hash of Google's stable account identifier to apply per-user daily limits and prevent abuse. It does not collect or store Google passwords, email addresses, raw Google account identifiers, or ID tokens. It may temporarily cache the submitted Tagalog sentence and its English result so identical requests do not require another translation.

Translation use is subject to initial limits of 25 new translations per Google account per UTC day and 200 new translations globally per UTC day. Cached translations do not consume an additional daily quota. These limits may change as capacity and usage require.

Purpose, sharing and Google Limited Use

Google user data is used only to identify the selected account, provide user-requested progress backup, synchronization and restoration, and authorize user-requested English translations. DUOLIBEE does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. DUOLIBEE's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

The browser communicates directly with Google services for authorization, account information and Drive storage. Translation requests are sent to DUOLIBEE's developer-operated translation gateway. The operator does not receive your Google account's private Drive saves through that gateway.

Other service requests

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives normal web requests. Wikipedia quizzes fetch public article text from Wikimedia services. The Google sign-in library loads from Google, and the Ko-fi support widget loads from Ko-fi. These providers may receive network information such as your IP address and process requests under their own policies. Device speech uses the browser's speech service; its processing depends on your browser and chosen voice. DUOLIBEE does not itself upload an audio recording of you.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until removed from the browser. Reset all progress clears the main local progress record; it does not delete Google Drive snapshots, synchronization metadata or the separate local restore backup. Clearing this site's browser data removes these local records. Cloud sync creates new snapshots and currently does not automatically delete older snapshots.

Translation results cached in sessionStorage are removed when the browser tab or session ends, or when you clear the site's browser data. Disconnect stops the current browser connection and does not delete existing local or cloud saves. You can revoke the app's authorization through your Google Account connections settings. Removing app authorization is separate from deleting stored Drive data. Manage DUOLIBEE's hidden app data through Google Drive's application management options where available, or contact support for deletion guidance.

Your choices and security

You can use local-only learning without connecting Google, disconnect at any time, and choose local or cloud progress when a conflict is detected. You can also decline Google sign-in for translations and continue learning without English translations. Network requests to Google and DUOLIBEE services use HTTPS. Account and device security remain important; progress backups are not a guarantee against all data loss. Do not share access tokens or account credentials.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions or deletion guidance, email bthompson91@gmail.com. If you contact support, your email and information you choose to send are received for handling that request. This policy may be updated as app features change; the effective date will be revised.
