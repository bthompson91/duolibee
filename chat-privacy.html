Account and Chat data use — v0.22.0

Effective 25 October 2026. This supplements the main privacy policy. For learning-progress erasure see the reset notice.

Account and backup

One Google sign-in supplies identity for DUOLIBEE and Chat. Google separately grants Drive app-data permission for the same account. Backup is automatic when authorised and online, with no off toggle. Denied or expired permission leaves backup pending and progress saved locally. Tokens remain in browser memory, not deliberately in localStorage, chat records or cloud saves. Signing out does not delete progress, snapshots or conversations.

Adult Chat in Australia

Chat no longer requires an email invitation. Google authentication, session ownership and usage limits still apply. Chat is offered to adults aged 18+ using it in Australia. Google sign-in and the Settings checkbox do not verify age or location. Untick the device-only checkbox if eligibility changes. Unchecking hides Chat and stops new browser requests, not already accepted server work. The backend verifies the Google ID token and stores the stable Google identifier for ownership and quotas. This is not unrestricted worldwide or all-ages access.

Dialogue, Gemini and storage

Common introductory questions about the bee's fictional name and origin can be answered locally. Question-only turns are unscored and do not require Gemini; mixed task answers still require assessment. This remains a mission-based introduction activity, not general-purpose free-form chat. If a bee reply supplies a full language example before a goal is completed, subsequent completion of that goal is marked assisted.

Replies requiring assessment, recent messages, trusted goals and instructions go to Gemini. Under unpaid-service terms, content and outputs may be human-reviewed and used to improve Google products. Use fictional details only; never submit sensitive, confidential or personal information, credentials, health or financial data. See Google's terms. No paid-provider/model fallback is implemented; request caps are not a billing guarantee.

The VM stores messages, ratings, hints, evidence, usage counters and cached replies in SQLite. Logs may contain IPs, paths, times and status codes. The browser stores account-scoped conversation IDs and the visibility preference, not deliberately transcripts or drafts. Chat is not included in Drive learning-progress snapshots. Stickers are assessment reactions, not extra AI calls. AI ratings may be wrong; Chat changes no XP, hearts or lesson rewards.

Limits and deletion

Per account: ten assessment attempts per UTC day, three per minute, five conversations. Shared: thirty attempts per UTC day, five per minute, twenty conversations, plus web-server limits. Failed provider calls count. Delete a loaded conversation through Chat options to remove it and cached replies from the live database. Usage counters remain. Inactive sessions older than seven days are purged when a new session is created, not by a guaranteed scheduled task. Manual backups and provider records are separate and not automatically deleted. Clearing browser data removes pointers, not server records. Contact bthompson91@gmail.com for backup or identifier deletion.
