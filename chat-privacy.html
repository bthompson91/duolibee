Conversations beta: privacy and data use

Effective 24 October 2026 · v0.19.0. This supplements the main privacy policy for Conversations.

Eligibility and authentication

This is an invitation-only adult beta approved for use in Australia. Eligibility is operator-confirmed, not established by Google sign-in or automatic location checks. Do not use it outside Australia. Google ID tokens are sent over HTTPS to the developer-operated Oracle backend for verification and invitation checking. The stable Google account identifier owns conversations and applies limits; verified email is checked against the invitation. Chat sign-in requests no Drive permissions. Tokens remain in browser memory and are not deliberately persisted in localStorage, chat records or Drive saves.

Gemini processing

Replies, recent conversation messages, mission goals and instructions are sent to Google Gemini for assessment. Under unpaid-service terms, submitted content and responses may be used to improve Google products and machine-learning technologies and reviewed by humans. Use fictional names and places only. Do not submit sensitive, confidential, personal, health or financial information, credentials, or Google tokens. Provider processing and retention follow Google's Gemini API terms. No paid-provider or model fallback is implemented; the operator must keep the provider project on the intended unpaid configuration. Request limits are not a billing guarantee.

Storage, limits and deletion

The VM stores messages, assessments, hints, goal evidence, ownership, usage counters and cached request replies in SQLite. Ordinary logs may contain IP addresses, paths, times and response codes. The browser stores conversation IDs to resume on the same device, not deliberately transcripts or drafts. Chat data is not added to learning-progress Drive snapshots. Chat changes no XP, hearts or lesson unlocks.

Initial limits: ten assessment attempts per account per UTC day, three per minute, and five saved conversations per account. Shared caps are thirty assessment attempts per UTC day, five per minute, twenty stored conversations, plus web-server rate limits. Failed provider calls consume allowance.

Delete a loaded conversation in Review to remove its messages and cached replies from the live database. Usage counters remain for abuse prevention. Sessions inactive for more than seven days are purged when a new session is created, not by a guaranteed scheduled purge. Manual operator backups can retain earlier copies and are not automatically erased by deletion. Provider records are separate. Contact support about backup or account-identifier deletion. Clearing browser data removes device pointers, not server records; disconnecting clears the browser chat credential, not conversations.

Your choice

Chat is optional. Accept the notice before creating conversations, sending replies or requesting hints. AI scores can be wrong. Lessons and reviews work without chat. Reloading the page requires chat sign-in again. Contact bthompson91@gmail.com.
