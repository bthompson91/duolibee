Progress erasure — v0.21.0

Effective 25 October 2026. This supersedes earlier local-only reset descriptions in the privacy policy.

The Settings erasure button requires an online Google connection and confirmation of the named account. It permanently deletes all DUOLIBEE learning-progress snapshots named duolibee-progress-v1.json from that account's private Drive app-data folder, and clears learning progress and restore copies on this device. Ordinary Drive documents, app preferences and Chat conversations are not deleted.

A minimal reset-generation marker and a new empty progress snapshot remain so updated devices cannot merge older progress back in. Publishing the feature does not perform erasure. Partial failures pause backup for that account and require retrying the erasure button; it does not claim success after a failed deletion.

Close other devices before erasing. Older app versions must be reloaded before reuse: they may still upload old snapshots, although updated clients ignore snapshots from earlier reset generations. This does not remotely wipe offline browser storage or separate server/operator/provider Chat records. Other devices apply the reset when the updated app next connects.
